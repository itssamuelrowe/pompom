# Pompom - What's New

_Released October 29, 2026_

A roundup of the features we shipped to make your focus sessions better.

## Track your progress with a new Analytics Dashboard

See how your focus time adds up. The new dashboard shows your sessions over time with clean, colorful charts, breaks down where your time goes by category, and highlights your daily activity at a glance. You can even share a snapshot of your stats.

## Build and keep your focus streaks

A new streak view celebrates your consistency, showing a calendar-style heatmap of your active days so you can watch your momentum grow.

## Overtime, your way

Went past the timer and kept going? Pompom now counts your overtime, saves a history of it, and gives you a compact workspace view and mini timer so you can keep an eye on things without losing your flow.

## Gentle reminders so you never drift off

Step away for too long and Pompom will give you a friendly nudge with optional notifications and sounds, helping you get back on track.

## More ways to personalize

Expanded settings let you fine-tune reminders, set up reusable session templates, and adjust sound volume so Pompom fits the way you like to work.

## Install Pompom like an app

Pompom is now installable on your device. Add it to your home screen or desktop, and it even works offline, just like a native app.

## Built-in help, right where you need it

New in-app documentation walks you through getting started, settings, and best practices, so answers are always a click away.

## A smoother, more connected experience

We rebuilt how you move around the app with a refreshed navigation bar and new pages, plus a short "why we built this" story so you can get to know Pompom better.
