## What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management method that breaks work into focused intervals (traditionally 25 minutes) separated by short breaks. After a set number of intervals you take a longer break. The fixed rhythm reduces the friction of starting and helps protect attention from constant context-switching.

::: callout
**Stored on your device.** PomPom keeps your settings and overtime history in your browser (localStorage), so your rhythm is remembered between visits.
:::
