# Settings reference

Open Settings from the gear icon in the top bar. Everything is saved in your browser and restored on reload.

## Format and durations

::: settings
- **Format** Pick a preset of durations, or choose **Custom** to set your own.
- **Pomodoro / Short break / Long break** The length in minutes of each mode (1 to 180).
- **Pomodoros before long break** How many pomodoros make up a cycle before a long break is scheduled.
:::

## Sound

::: settings
- **Ringtone** The tone played when a session ends. Preview it before committing.
- **Volume** Playback level for all synthesized sounds.
- **Keep ringing until stopped** When on, the end-of-session alarm loops until you press Stop Ringing. When off, it plays briefly and stops on its own.
:::

## Behavior

::: settings
- **Auto-cycle sessions** Automatically move between Pomodoro, Short Break and Long Break in order.
- **Auto-start next timer** Start the next session automatically when one finishes, with no clicks. Default off.
:::

## Reminders

::: settings
- **Idle reminders** While PomPom is open and the timer is not running, send a browser notification to nudge you back to focus. Requires notification permission. The panel shows the current permission status and a button to grant it.
- **Remind every** Minutes between idle reminders (1 to 120).
- **Funny random messages** Each reminder pulls a random quip from a pack of nearly 100 instead of a fixed message. Example: "This little maneuver is going to cost us 5 minutes."
- **Reminder message** Your custom reminder text, used when funny messages are off.
- **Also remind when a session ends** Send a notification the moment a pomodoro or break finishes, in addition to the idle interval.
- **Idle sound** Play a short two-beat chime on a loop while the timer is idle. Separate from the ringtone, it never rings continuously. Configure how often it plays, and preview it (which also unlocks audio).
:::

::: callout
Browsers cannot wake a closed tab on a schedule, so reminders fire while PomPom is open. Installing the app and granting notification permission gives the most reliable delivery.
:::

## Appearance

::: settings
- **Theme** Light, Dark, or System (follows your device preference).
- **Accent color** Choose from a curated palette. It recolors the whole interface.
:::

## Overtime history

Open it from the history icon in the top bar. Each time you work past a timer and then move to the next session, the overrun is logged with its mode and time. Remove individual entries or clear the whole log. History is stored only in your browser.

## Data

::: settings
- **Reset to defaults** Restore all settings to their original values.
:::
